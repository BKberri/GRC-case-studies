# 2026-08-EU-AI-Act-Transparency-Enforcement
**Date:** 2026-08-03 | **Source:** EU-AI-Act | **Category:** AI-Governance | **Risk Rating:** High

## Summary
The EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations became actively enforced on 2026-08-02, requiring disclosure of AI interaction (chatbots/agents), deepfake labeling, and machine-readable watermarking of generative AI outputs — applicable regardless of an AI system's high-risk classification status, and independent of the Digital Omnibus's separate delay of high-risk system obligations to December 2027. Non-compliance carries fines up to €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover under Article 99. California's SB 942 (AI Transparency Act) took effect the same day, creating overlapping US obligations for the same product surfaces. NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001 apply as the governance framework track. Status: compliance implementation (disclosure UI, watermarking, attestation) in progress per POA&M.
