# Executive Summary
## 2026-08-EU-AI-Act-Transparency-Enforcement
**Date:** 2026-08-03 | **Prepared by:** GRC Intelligence Program | **Classification:** High

## What Happened
As of August 2, 2026, European regulators began actively enforcing new rules requiring any AI system that talks to customers, or that generates images, video, audio, or text, to clearly disclose that it's AI. This applies now, separately from — and ahead of — the EU's stricter "high-risk AI" rules, which were pushed back to late 2027.

## Why It Matters
If we have any AI-powered chatbot, customer-facing agent, or content-generation feature reaching EU users, we are now required to disclose it as AI-generated or AI-driven. Fines for non-compliance can reach 3% of our global annual revenue. A similar disclosure law also took effect in California on the same date, so this is a two-jurisdiction compliance question, not just a European one.

## What We Are Doing About It
- Inventory every customer-facing AI feature and content-generation tool that reaches EU or California users (GRC Program + Product, target: 2 weeks)
- Implement clear AI-interaction disclosure in any chatbot or conversational AI interface (Product Engineering, target: 30 days)
- Implement machine-readable watermarking on AI-generated content outputs (Product Engineering, target: 60–90 days)
- Prepare a compliance attestation document for enterprise customer procurement/security reviews that increasingly ask about this (GRC Program, target: 30 days)

## Bottom Line
This is a compliance deadline that already passed for anything not yet in place — the priority is closing the gap quickly and documenting the plan, since enterprise customers are starting to ask about this in vendor security reviews regardless of whether regulators come knocking first.
